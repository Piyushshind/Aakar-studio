# Credits

Everything the Aakar Digital Photo Studio website uses that was not made for it, with its
licence (spec 7.1 and 16). Update this file whenever a photo source, font or library is added.

## Photographs

All photographs on the site are the studio's own work, supplied by the owner (Prakash S.
Suryawanshi, Aakar Digital Photo Studio, Jalgaon), and are published with the consent of the
families in them (spec 6). They are not licensed for reuse.

- **Studio photos:** every entry in `src/data/photos.manifest.json` has `source: "studio"`.
- **Stock photos:** none are used. If one is ever added (backgrounds and atmosphere only,
  never the portfolio), list it here with its source page and licence, and give its manifest
  entry `credit` and `licenseUrl`.
- **AI-generated images:** none, anywhere. One owner portrait (`about-owner-01`) was supplied
  with a small Gemini mark from an editing app; it is a real photograph, published as is by the
  developer's decision (2026-10-26).

## Logo and brand assets

The logo (`src/assets/brand/`, `public/favicon/`, `public/og-image.jpg`,
`src/components/ui/logoArt.ts`) is hand-built vector art redrawn for the studio from its
banner, generated by `scripts/brand/build.mjs`. The wordmark and tagline are outlined from
Fraunces and Manrope (below).
